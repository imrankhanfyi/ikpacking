So, I occasionally get weirdly sad and annoyed that we were (or more specifically, I was) born too late to see the dinosaurs, but too early to meet the aliens

I mean, rubbish timing, right? Like, I’m not being greedy. I don’t need both. But at least one would have been nice

But then I remember that we’re alive at the same time as the blue whale. It’s not just the biggest animal alive right now… it’s the biggest animal to have *ever* been alive. Like, ever.

For all we know, it could be the largest sentient creature that the entire universe has ever seen. I mean, it’s unlikely. But we don’t know that it’s not. And we’re around to see it!

Anyway, point is, I know it looks like a giant ballsack, but this is the preserved heart of a blue whale. It’s the biggest heart! Possibly in the universe. You could fit inside it. Ladder for scale.
